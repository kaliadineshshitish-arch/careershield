Privacy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · CareerShield is a DK Digital Studio product

The short version: the checks and the tracker run inside your browser and nothing is stored on our side. The AI tools send only the text you paste to our AI provider so it can answer, and we do not keep it. Payments are handled by Razorpay — we never see your card or UPI details.

What stays on your device

  • Resume PDFs — read in your browser with pdf.js. The file is never uploaded.
  • Scam check answers, ATS text, job match text — used in the page and then gone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Saved resume versions — stored in this browser as cs_versions_v1.
  • Job application tracker — stored in this browser as cs_apps_v1. Use “Export file” to keep a backup.
  • Licence key and AI call count — cs_licence_v1 and cs_quota_v1, so you do not have to paste the key again.

Clearing your browser's site data deletes all of the above. Nothing can be recovered from our side, because we never had it.

What leaves your device

  • AI tools: the text you type into an AI tool (resume text, job description, career-gap reason, answers) is sent to our server function and from there to Sarvam AI, an Indian AI provider, which generates the reply. We do not store that text in a database and we do not log it. Sarvam processes it to produce the answer under its own terms.
  • Your IP address is used for rate limiting (to stop abuse of the AI credits) and is kept in a counter table for up to two days.
  • Page and tool counts: we keep a simple first-party counter so we know which tools people actually use — one row per page load with the page, the referring site, the device type and a one-way hash of your IP. No cookies, no third-party analytics script, no profile of you. Rows older than 90 days are deleted automatically.
  • Payments: Razorpay processes the payment. We receive a payment reference, the plan and the amount — never your card, UPI ID or bank details.
  • Licence records: we store a one-way hash of your licence key, the plan, how many AI calls were used and the expiry date. The key itself cannot be read back from the hash.
  • Reviews: if you post a review, your first name, star rating and comment are stored and published after we check them. Do not write anything personal in a review.

What we do not do

  • No account, no email signup, no password.
  • No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics scripts, no cross-site pixels.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone other than the providers listed above, who only process it to deliver the service.

Your choices

  • Delete local data: clear site data in your browser, or delete entries inside the tracker.
  • Ask us to erase a review or a licence record: email us from the address below with the reference; we will delete it.
  • Ask what we hold about you: email us and we will tell you, usually within a few days.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask for access, correction or erasure of your personal data. Write to us and we will act on it.

Children

CareerShield is meant for people 18 and over who are looking for work. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
